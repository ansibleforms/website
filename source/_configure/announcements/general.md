## Turning an announcement on or off

From 7.3.0 each announcement is turned on or off on its own, with its **Enabled** switch.

A new announcement is enabled. **Disable** in its row menu, or the **Enabled** switch on its page, hides it under the header and keeps
it in the list, and **Enable** shows it again from its start date until its end date. The switch is saved at once.

---

## The Show announcements switch of 7.2

Up to 7.2, a **General** tab had a **Show announcements** switch that turned all of them on or off at once.

From 7.3.0 that switch is gone. When it was off, the upgrade disables every announcement, so none starts to show: enable the ones you want again.
