# Account
{: .no_toc }

Who you are signed in as
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

---

The Account view shows who you are signed in as and what determines your access:

- **Username** and **Login type**: a local account, LDAP, Azure AD or OpenID Connect.
- **Groups**: the groups that come with your login, such as `local/admins` or `ldap/Domain Admins`.
- **Roles**: the roles in `config.yaml` that match those groups and your user. They determine
  which forms you see and which options you have.
